## Top-loading

### Agitator

- Have a pole in the middle
- Tougher on fabrics
- Can damage delicate items
- Less expensive than impeller

### Impeller

- Easier/gentler on the clothes
- Slightly longer delivery time
- Low profile cone or disk at the bottom
