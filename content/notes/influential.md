## Influential.co - The Unrivaled Global Leader in Influencer Marketing

Influential is the largest influencer marketing company and platform in the
world. Its proprietary AI-powered technology platform with over 100 billion data
points, and its network of over 3.5M creators including 90% of global
influencers with 1M+ followers, are at the service of 300+ brands around the
world.

_At Influential, we help brands connect with influencers_, by:

- sealing contracts,
- curating content,
- tracking campaigns.

At its core, the company is a medium by which brands can _activate influencers
of their choosing_. Influential’s differentiated offering enables brands to
source high-quality digital creators, curate impactful creative strategy, and
activate, amplify and optimize digital media to drive real-world outcomes. We
provide these capabilities through a SAAS platform as well as a managed service.
