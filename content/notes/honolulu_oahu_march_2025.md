## Accommodation

- 6 nights, 7 days
- Recommended to stay on Waikiki Beach
  - Average between $175 - 300+ per night. Mostly apartments.

## Weather

- The average weather in March in Oahu is high 78 degrees F and low 64 degrees
  F, with rain 5 days of the month.
- There are 12 hours of daylight.
- The average humidity is around 70%
- The average sunset time in March in Oahu is 6:30 PM

## Mobility

### Traffic

- Real-time Oahu traffic monitoring: [GoAkamai](http://goakamai.org/)

### TheBus

> "TheBus is Oahu’s public bus system. You can catch a ride for $3.00 to pretty
> much anywhere on the island. You can also pick up a HOLO card which costs $2,
> and works similarly to an Oyster Card. To use the HOLO card, you just need to
> add money to it, and then tap into the bus. After paying for two rides, you’ll
> then be eligible for a day pass and the rest of your trips will be free for
> the day."

| Fare Type   | Price  | Details                                                      |
| ----------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Single Fare | $3.00  | Includes 2.5 hours unlimited transfers                       |
| Day Cap     | $7.50  | No additional payments after this amount is reached in a day |
| 3-Day Pass  | $20.00 | Valid 72 hours from first use                                |
| 7-Day Pass  | $35.00 | Valid 168 hours from first use                               |

(from TheBus transit pass website)

For our trip, getting a Holo Card + 4x 7-day passes seems to be the most
economical.

## Must Do Activities

### Tours

- Circle Island Tour. Great for getting a lay of the land. $125 per person.
  Recommended to do early in the trip.

  - Nu'uanu Pali Lookout
  - Macadamia Nut Farm
  - Waimea Bay / Valley in North Shore
  - Mokoli'i Island at Kualoa Park Beach

### Museums + Historic Centers

- Pearl Harbor + Aviation Museum. Recommended a whole day.
- Historic Honolulu in Downtown HNL
  - 'Iolani Palace
  - Royal Palace of the Kingdom of Hawai'i
  - Arts district

### Waikiki Rest and Relax

- Waikiki Beach
- Surfing, standup padde boarding, sailboat tours, HNL zoo
- Plenty to do for 2 days.
- Extremely walkable

### North Shore

- Hale'iwa town

### East O'ahu

- Diamond Head via 2 Bus (25-30 minutes away). $5 to enter walking per person.

  - Hours: 6 AM to 6 PM
  - TODO: Reserve a time slot
  - Hike is about 1 hour out and back
  - Earlier we go the better since it's very busy

- Kaimuki neighborhood. "Eccentric town with lots of good food." 30 minute walk
  from Diamond Head or 6 minute bus ride. Lots of vegan/veg options on Waialae
  Avenue.
- Koko Head District Park + Botanical Gardens
- China Walls (about an hour of walking + bus from koko head). Great place to
  watch the sunset. 1 hr bus back to waikiki.
- Kailua + Lanikai beach. 1.5 hours away from Waikiki by bus. White sand beach
  and chill water. Best place to snorkel.

### Surfing Lessons

- [Pro Surf School Hawaii](https://www.prosurfschoolhawaii.com/beginner-surf-lessons-waikiki.html)
  - 9a-11am, 12p-2pm, 3pm-5pm.
  - Group lessons up to 6 people are $100 for 2 hours.
