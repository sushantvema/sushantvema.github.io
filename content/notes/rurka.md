## Follow Up Call Sat April 19 2025

### Questions For Rurka

#### Upgrades & Pricing

- What upgrades come included at the base price vs. which are considered premium or optional?
- Are there any upgrade packages or bulk upgrade discounts for early buyers?
- Can we customize the layout or finishes, and what are the associated costs?
- Are there any upcoming changes in material or design standards that could impact pricing?

#### Incentives & Financing

- What early-buyer incentives or discounts are currently offered, and how long do they last?
- Are there closing cost assistance programs available?
- Can you explain the specific incentives tied to the Model 2 plan?
- What loan products does the preferred lender offer, and what are the current interest rates?
- Are there rate buy-down options through the developer or lender?
- Are there any lender-specific incentives, such as gift cards, lower fees, or appraisal exemptions?

#### Construction & Timeline

- What is the projected completion date for the home at 418? For subsequent phases like 325 or 345?
- What contingencies are in place if the builder faces construction delays?
- How and when will we be updated on construction progress?
- Are there any upcoming development phases planned beyond the initial releases?

#### Solar & Sustainability

- Is solar standard or optional, and what is the additional cost if optional?
- Who owns the solar system (owned vs. leased)?
- Are there battery storage options? What happens with solar incentives or tax credits?
- Are there any green building certifications or energy efficiency standards the homes comply with?

#### Community & Miscellaneous

- What amenities or community features are included in the development? (Parks, trails, clubhouses)
- Are HOA fees applicable? What do they cover and how much are they?
- How is future community growth planned? Will new phases add traffic or impact property value?
- What is the process for warranty issues or post-sale support?
- Are there any restrictions or covenants on lot use or exterior modifications?
